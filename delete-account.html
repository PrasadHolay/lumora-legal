Account Management

Request Account Deletion

We're sorry to see you go. You can request deletion of your Lumora account and all associated data at any time.

What will be deleted

  • Your account and login credentials
  • Skin analysis results and facial data
  • Personalisation quiz responses and preferences
  • Skincare and makeup recommendation history
  • Order history and cart data
  • Wishlist and saved products
  • Progress tracking data

Please note

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Any active subscriptions should be cancelled before requesting deletion. We will process your request within 30 days.

How to delete your account

1

Delete directly in the app (fastest)

Open Lumora and go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This will immediately begin the deletion process.

2

Or send us an email request

Email us with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and include the email address associated with your Lumora account. We will confirm deletion within 30 days.

3

Confirmation

You will receive an email confirmation once your account and all associated data has been permanently deleted.

Send your deletion request to our support team. Include the email address registered with your Lumora account.

Request Account Deletion admin@lumorabeauty.co.uk

Cancel your subscription first

If you have an active Lumora Premium subscription, please cancel it before requesting account deletion. You can manage your subscription via the App Store or Google Play.
